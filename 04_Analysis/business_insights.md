# Financial Budget & Variance Analysis — Business Insights

## 1. Overall Budget Performance

In 2025, the total budget was $8,870,684.76, while actual spending was $9,095,144.35.

This means the company spent $224,459.59 more than planned, which is 2.53% over budget.

Overall, the company went over its budget for the year.

## 2. Cost Centre Performance

Facilities had the largest amount over budget. It spent $68,881.25 more than planned, which was 5.65% over budget.

Data and Analytics had the highest percentage over budget. It spent 6.12% more than planned, equal to $57,502.00.

Other cost centres that went over budget were:

- Legal: $41,139.55 over budget (4.45%)
- Operations: $33,087.99 over budget (4.14%)
- People: $31,746.89 over budget (3.19%)
- Finance: $8,724.80 over budget (1.02%)
- Sales: $8,668.44 over budget (0.97%)
- Marketing: $521.82 over budget (0.08%)

Engineering and Customer Support were the only cost centres that stayed below budget.

Engineering spent $19,018.78 less than its budget, while Customer Support spent $6,794.37 less.

This shows why both the amount and percentage are useful. Facilities had the biggest overspending amount, while Data and Analytics had the highest overspending percentage.

## 3. Data and Analytics

Data and Analytics spent $57,502.00 more than its total budget, or 6.12% over budget.

Salaries had the largest overspending amount at $15,940.00.

Other categories that went over budget were:

- Contractors: $12,296.42 over budget (10.87%)
- Cloud hosting: $9,549.31 over budget (10.74%)
- Software subscriptions: $9,239.95 over budget (12.04%)
- Marketing programmes: $7,932.82 over budget (7.85%)
- Facilities: $3,972.29 over budget (6.15%)

Software subscriptions had the highest overspending percentage at 12.04%.

Training and Travel stayed below budget.

This means Salaries contributed the largest amount to the overspending, while Software subscriptions had the largest percentage over budget.

## 4. Facilities

Facilities spent $68,881.25 more than planned, making it the cost centre with the largest overspending amount.

Salaries had the largest overspending amount within Facilities at $24,036.92.

Other categories that went over budget were:

- Contractors: $14,446.72 over budget (9.84%)
- Cloud hosting: $11,256.64 over budget (9.75%)
- Software subscriptions: $11,135.12 over budget (11.17%)
- Marketing programmes: $8,450.74 over budget (6.44%)
- Facilities: $4,209.12 over budget (5.01%)

Software subscriptions had the highest overspending percentage at 11.17%.

Training and Travel stayed below budget.

The results show that several expense categories contributed to the Facilities overspending, with Salaries having the largest amount.

## 5. Monthly Budget Performance

Actual spending was higher than the budget in every month of 2025.

Some months had larger differences between budget and actual spending than others.

Because overspending happened throughout the year, the overall result was not caused by only one month.

This shows why checking budget performance every month is useful. It can help management notice overspending earlier.

## 6. Key Findings

1. The company spent $224,459.59 more than its 2025 budget, which is 2.53% over budget.

2. Facilities had the largest overspending amount at $68,881.25.

3. Data and Analytics had the highest overspending percentage at 6.12%.

4. Engineering and Customer Support were the only cost centres that stayed below budget.

5. Salaries had the largest overspending amount within both Facilities and Data and Analytics.

6. Software subscriptions had a high overspending percentage in both Facilities and Data and Analytics.

7. Actual spending was higher than budget in every month of 2025.

## 7. Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Management should review Facilities and Data and Analytics first because they had the largest budget differences.

2. The company should look more closely at Salaries, Contractors, Cloud hosting, and Software subscriptions because these categories contributed to the overspending.

3. Budget and actual spending should be checked every month so that overspending can be noticed earlier.

4. The company could set alerts when spending goes too far above budget.

5. Future budgets should consider categories that often go over budget so that planned amounts are more realistic.

6. The company could also review Engineering and Customer Support to understand how they managed to stay below budget.

## 8. Conclusion

The 2025 analysis shows that the company spent $224,459.59 more than planned, or 2.53% over budget.

Facilities had the largest overspending amount, while Data and Analytics had the highest overspending percentage.

The analysis also shows that Salaries, Contractors, Cloud hosting, and Software subscriptions were important areas to review.

By checking budget performance by cost centre, expense category, and month, management can better understand where spending is above plan and make better budgeting decisions.
